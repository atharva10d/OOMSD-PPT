The Proposal

What are we proposing?

We propose a comprehensive, Object-Oriented Airline Reservation System that:

Automates real-time flight search and dynamic seat allocation.
Provides role-specific secure portals — Admin, Travel Agent, Passenger.
Integrates external Payment Gateways for secure, instant transactions.
Manages the entire lifecycle: booking, web check-in and boarding-pass generation.
Maintains a centralised, collision-free database to prevent overbooking.
