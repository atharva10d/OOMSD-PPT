Section 03

SMART Objectives

Five commitments that make the outcome specific, measurable and reviewable.

S

Specific

Design a computer vision pipeline to accurately localize and classify manufacturing defects while integrating Generative AI for root cause analysis.

M

Measurable

Automate the creation of comprehensive quality inspection reports to track defect metrics and reduce administrative burden.

A

Achievable

Develop a centralized dashboard with an interactive natural language chatbot for real-time production statistics.

R

Relevant

Target high-criticality automobile parts to prevent defects from reaching the assembly line, ensuring quality assurance.

T

Time-bound

Complete data collection, model training, dashboard development and system validation within the allotted academic timeline.
