Problem Identification
& Relevance

Quality control on the automotive floor is still bottlenecked by human eyes and rigid rules.

Human throughput & attention limited

Manual Inspection
Flaws

Quality control relies on manual inspection, which is slow and prone to fatigue — consistency drops across long shifts and micro-defects slip through.

No adaptation to new conditions

Legacy System
Flaws

Rigid rule-based machine vision fails under varying lighting and complex geometries — thresholds tuned for one part break on the next.

Failures reach the end user

Impact &
Consequences

Undetected micro-defects — hairline cracks, surface rust — lead to catastrophic failures, safety hazards and expensive product recalls.

System Alert  [⚠] → 

Existing automated systems flag defects but lack "Diagnostic Context" to explain why the defect occurred.
