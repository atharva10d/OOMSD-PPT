Literature Review & Need Analysis

Three method families surveyed — each efficient in isolation, none diagnostic.

Methodology
Limitations & Research Gaps

Hybrid CNN–Transformer Models

  • Computationally expensive for edge deployment
  • Limited generalization across different industrial domains
  • Rely heavily on large, annotated datasets

Lightweight YOLOv8-Based Detector (ACMSP-YOLO)

  • Evaluated mainly on steel surface datasets (applicability to diverse parts not validated)
  • Restricted strictly to 2D image-based inspection

General AI, Deep Learning & Foundation Models

  • Depend heavily on massive, labeled datasets
  • Require pixel-level annotation for segmentation (highly expensive and time-consuming)

The Research Gap

A critical need exists for an intelligent system that not only detects surface flaws but utilizes Generative AI for automated root cause analysis and natural language operator assistance.
