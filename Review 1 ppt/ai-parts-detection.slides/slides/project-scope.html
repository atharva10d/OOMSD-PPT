Section 04

Project Scope & Boundaries

What the system will inspect — and what is deliberately left out of this phase.

In Scope

Target Components

Inspection focuses on high-criticality automobile parts within the main assembly block.

Gears Brake Discs Pistons Engine Blocks

In Scope

Defect Categories

Identifies diverse physical manufacturing faults using surface analysis.

Cracks Dents Scratches Rust Dimensional Faults

In Scope • Operational Environment

Designed to simulate real-time production line operations, analyzing images actively without disrupting the overall manufacturing workflow.

Out of Scope

Exclusions

The current phase is heavily focused on vision modeling and is intentionally limited to static image captures.

Does not process continuous live video feeds. No integration with robotic hardware for physical part removal.
